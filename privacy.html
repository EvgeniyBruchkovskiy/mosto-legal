Privacy Policy

Effective: October 4, 2026.

Mosto is a cooperative bridge-building game. We collect as little as possible.

Solo play

Solo play works offline. Your stars, settings and nickname are stored only on your device.

Playing together

When you create or join a room, the app signs in anonymously with Google Firebase. Firebase assigns a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. To run the shared game, the room stores your nickname, chosen color, the pieces you place, reactions and run results. This data is used only to play the game and is deleted when the room is cleaned up (rooms older than 24 hours are removed).

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data. We do not collect contacts, location, photos or voice.

Children

Mosto contains no chat; players communicate only with five preset emoji and map markers.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: thewayprod1@gmail.com.
